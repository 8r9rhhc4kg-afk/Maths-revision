📄 Know your papers

Paper 1: Non-calculator — 40 marks.

Paper 2: Calculator — 50 marks.

The two papers are worth 90 marks in total. Practise showing clear working, not just writing the final answer.

Official National 5 Mathematics course resources ↗

🧠 Quick exam reminders

  • Read the question carefully.
  • Write down the formula before substituting values.
  • Keep enough decimal places during working.
  • Check units and rounding instructions.
  • If stuck, move on and return later.
